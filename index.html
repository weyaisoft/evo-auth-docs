Evo Auth

A self-hosted sign-in system for web apps. It signs people in, keeps them signed in with first-party cookies, and issues short-lived signed tokens that your backend accepts as proof of who they are.

Start with where your auth runs

  • COn ConvexInstall the component, mount the routes, set the variables and rotate the signing keys.
  • SOn its own serverOne Node process and one SQLite file: install, options, a proxy in front, webhooks and backups.
  • TTanStack StartThe app side: the proxy route, middleware, the nonce and the content security policy.
  • HAnother runtimeMount the handler on Hono, Bun, Deno or Node with your own storage.
